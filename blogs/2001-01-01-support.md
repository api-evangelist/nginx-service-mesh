F5 NGINX Gateway Fabric adheres to the support policy detailed in the following knowledge base article: K000140156 . After opening a support ticket, F5 staff will request additional information to better understand the problem. Kubernetes support plugin The nginx-supportpkg-for-k8s plugin collects the information needed by F5 Technical Support to assist with troubleshooting your issue.
